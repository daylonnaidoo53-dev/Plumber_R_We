A few things you might ask.

We offer free, no-obligation quotes for standard jobs. For after-hours emergency callouts, a callout fee may apply — we'll always tell you upfront before we come out.

For emergencies in and around Paarl, we aim to be on-site within a few hours. For standard bookings, we typically schedule within 24–48 hours.

Yes — we operate Saturdays and are available for emergency callouts on Sundays and public holidays.
